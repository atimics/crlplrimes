## Takeaway

The current promotions keep the fixed ARC certificate intact and move public-evaluation coverage from a separator-compression win to a larger lattice copy/extend win. Grammar v15 keeps that coverage but also routes the lattice verifier through `NNHypothesis` induction, so the proposer can learn and rank the verifier template separately from the static candidate. The remaining ARC gap is not capacity; it is unsupported symbolic family coverage.
